Live a Square Life

Level Seven is a mentorship and life development program for youth and adults choosing a better path. We believe recovery is more than overcoming substance addiction. It’s about breaking free from anything that holds you back — negative influences, poor decision-making, mental and emotional struggles, or hopelessness.

Through mentorship, education, sports training, life coaching, and community partnerships, we help participants build confidence, discipline, leadership, and the skills needed to create lasting, positive change. Our philosophy ‘Live a Square Life’ means choosing integrity, responsibility, respect, and purpose every day. No matter where your journey begins, Level Seven is here to help you connect, grow, recover, and succeed.

Who Level Seven is for

Young people who want structure, mentors, and something worth working toward. Adults rebuilding life after substance use. Returning citizens. Anyone choosing a healthier path.
