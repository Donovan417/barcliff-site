What Barcliff coordinates

You bring your strength — hiring, housing, skilled work, time, or programming. We handle the connective work in between.

Participants arrive with mentorship and support behind them, coordination runs through one reliable contact, and your work translates directly into jobs, homes, and better paths in your own neighborhood.
